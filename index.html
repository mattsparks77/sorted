Short levels. Big combos.

Chain matching items, defuse batteries, and keep the pile from toppling over.

Every country, new trash

Five countries to clean up, each with its own cities and its own litter.

Two ways to play

Story

Travel city by city and earn up to three stars on every level. Finish a city to stamp it on your board, then move on to the next country.

Survival

Endless waves that keep speeding up, with three hearts to lose. Every run earns stars you can spend to unlock new countries.

  • Tap 3+ matching items for a combo
  • Scratch batteries to pop them for bonus points
  • Clear the pile before it turns into a bomb

Free to play, fair to play. You hold up to 3 lives. A story level only costs one if you lose it, and each Survival run uses one. Lives refill every 20 minutes, or you can watch a short ad for another.
