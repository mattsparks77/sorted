Privacy Policy

How Sorted! The Trash Game handles your information.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sparky Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Sorted! (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Summary

  • We do not require an account and do not ask you for your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Your game progress is stored locally on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain data (such as device identifiers) to provide and personalize ads.

Information we collect

Information you provide: None. The App has no accounts or sign-in.

Information stored on your device: Game progress, settings, high scores, and lives are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.

Information collected automatically by our advertising partner: To show ads, our third-party ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifiers (for example, Apple's IDFA, the Identifier for Advertisers)
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, network information, IP address)
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction and app usage data
  • Diagnostic and crash data

We ourselves do not receive or store this information in a form that identifies you.

How information is used

Information collected by our ad partner is used to:

  • Serve, deliver, and measure ads (including personalized ads where permitted)
  • Detect and prevent fraud and improve ad performance
  • Provide aggregated analytics

Advertising and your choices

App Tracking Transparency (iOS): On first launch, iOS asks whether you allow the App to track your activity across other apps and websites. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): If you are in these regions, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads. You can adjust your choices there.

Opt out of personalized ads:

  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising, plus the Tracking settings above.
  • Google / AdMob ad settings: adssettings.google.com

Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google's handling of data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act). Because we do not maintain accounts or personally identifiable records, most data associated with you is controlled by our ad partner; you can exercise choices through the ad settings linked above, or by uninstalling the App (which removes locally stored data). To make a request or ask questions, contact us below.

Data retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Data collected by our ad partner is retained according to Google's policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
